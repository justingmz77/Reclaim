    Wellness Tools

    Boost your wellness journal with these tools.

    4-7-8 Breathing (Calm-Down Reset)

    When to use: Before an exam, presentation, or when stress spikes

    1. Inhale through your nose for 4 seconds
    2. Hold for 7 seconds
    3. Exhale slowly through your mouth for 8 seconds

    🧘‍♀️ Slows the nervous system and reduces anxiety quickly

    The 2-Minute “Name 5” Grounding Exercise

    When to use: When your brain feels overwhelmed

    1. Identify 5 things you can see
    2. 4 things you can feel
    3. 2 things you can smell
    4. 1 thing you can taste

    🌳 Brings your focus to the present moment and calms racing thoughts

    Pomodoro with Mindful Pauses

    When to use: Studying, writing papers, or doing assignments

    1. Work for 25 minutes
    2. Take a 5-minute mindful break: no phone, just stretching, breathing, looking out a window
    3. After 4 rounds, take a longer 15–30 min break

    💭 Prevents burnout and increases focus by giving your brain structured rest

    Mindful Walking (5–10 Minutes)

    When to use: In between classes or when you need a refresh

    1. Walk slowly
    2. Pay attention to your feet touching the ground
    3. Notice sounds, temperature, and movement around you

    ☀️ Combines light physical activity with mental reset, which improves memory and mood
